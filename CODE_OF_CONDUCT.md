# Code of Conduct

This applies to every One Man's Junk repository, issue, pull request, and discussion.

## The short version

Be decent. Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume the other person means well, and help newcomers the way you'd want to be helped.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Spam, including advertising unrelated projects or services
- Posting cracked, "nulled," or pirated copies of our software, or links to them

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules, at their discretion. To report something, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or [report the user to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
